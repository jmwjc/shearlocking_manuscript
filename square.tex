\subsection{Square plate subjected non-uniform transverse load}
Consider a square plate with the clamped boundary, as showing in Figure. , subjecting a non-uniform transverse load $\bar p$:
\begin{equation}
\bar p = \frac{Eh^{3}}{12(1-\nu^{2})}\left(
    \begin{aligned}
        12y(y-1)(5x^{2}-5x+1)\bigl(2y^{2}(y-1)^{2}+x(x-1)(5y^{2}-5y+1)\bigr) \\ +12x(x-1)(5y^{2}-5y+1)\bigl(2x^{2}(x-1)^{2}+y(y-1)(5x^{2}-5x+1)\bigr)
    \end{aligned}
\right)
\end{equation}
In this circumstance, the exact solution of the deflection and rotation are listed as follows:
\begin{equation}
w = \frac{1}{3}x^{3}(x-1)^{3}y^{3}(y-1)^{3} - \frac{2h^{2}}{5(1-\nu)}
\left(
\begin{aligned}
&y^{3}(y-1)^{3}x(x-1)(5x^{2}-5x+1) + \\ 
&x^{3}(x-1)^{3}y(y-1)(5y^{2}-5y+1)
\end{aligned}
\right)
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
\varphi_{1} &= y^{3}(y-1)^{3}x^{2}(x-1)^{2}(2x-1) \\
\varphi_{2} &= x^{3}(x-1)^{3}y^{2}(y-1)^{2}(2y-1)
\end{aligned}
\right .
\end{equation}
